Our Story

Founded in 1998 by a group of educators, Meridian grew from 120 students to a multi-disciplinary institute known for research, ethics and employability. Affiliated to a state university and accredited NAAC A, AICTE approved, NBA accredited.

Vision

To be a globally respected centre of learning that transforms curious minds into responsible leaders.

Mission

Deliver outcome-based education, nurture research and serve society through innovation.

Core Values

🤝Integrity
